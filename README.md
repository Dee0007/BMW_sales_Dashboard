# 🚗 BMW Sales Performance & Target Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive executive **Power BI Dashboard** built to evaluate BMW sales performance, track total revenue against organizational targets, and analyze volume trends across car models, fuel types, and geographic regions.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Revenue:** 5T
* **Average Vehicle Price:** 74.70K
* **Total Sales Volume:** 65M units
* **Average Sales Volume:** 5.07K units
* **Revenue vs. Goal Target:** 0.35T achieved vs. 5T goal (-92.99%)
* **Sales Volume vs. Goal Target:** 4.65M achieved vs. 18M goal (-74.18%)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Visualizations
* **Yearly Sales Volume Trend:** Line chart illustrating historical sales volume trajectory over time to identify growth peaks and market cycles.
* **Top Selling Models:** Waterfall chart breaking down volume contributions, increases, decreases, and cumulative totals by car model.
* **Regional Distribution:** Donut chart analyzing the sum of sales volume across key regions (10M, 11M, 11M, 10M split).
* **Target Variance Analysis:** Gauge/card tracking progress toward set revenue and unit volume targets.
* **Dynamic Slicers:** Interactive filters for **Model** selection and **Fuel_Type** (Hybrid, Petrol, Electric, etc.).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Power BI Desktop:** Report layout design, custom dark theme UI with official BMW M-series styling.
* **Power Query & DAX:** Data transformation, target-variance calculation formulas, and KPI aggregation.
